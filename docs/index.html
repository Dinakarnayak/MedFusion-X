Research framework · Multimodal medical AI

Evidence-aware fusion for medical imaging.

MedFusion-X studies how image and text representations can be combined with adaptive gating, disagreement estimation and uncertainty-aware prediction for reproducible medical AI research.

Dataset

MedFusion-X supports the NIH ChestX-ray14 research dataset. The full medical image corpus is intentionally not committed to GitHub. Follow the repository's dataset setup instructions to download and validate it locally.

Responsible research

This project is for research and education, not clinical deployment. Model outputs require independent verification and should not be used as medical advice or diagnosis.
